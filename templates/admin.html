Net profit

You capture costs in person · salons never see this · Premium leverage

—

Proration: Rent, staff salary, and other monthly costs are stored as monthly amounts. For any date range they are scaled as monthly × (days in range ÷ 30). COGS uses actual service counts in the range × unit cost.

Psychology play — Your team visits the salon, learns rent / salaries / utilities / generator fuel. You enter costs here. Owners only see booking magic; you see true net profit. That insight is the Premium story: “We know if your salon is actually making money.”
Net = Revenue − COGS − Prorated expenses − Prorated staff salary
